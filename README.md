# 🚀✨ Flowery E-commerce App ✨🚀  

## 📢 About the Project  
Flowery is a modern **E-commerce app** designed to provide a seamless shopping experience for flower lovers.  
The app allows users to browse collections, purchase trending floral arrangements, and send gifts effortlessly.  

## 🎯 Key Features  

### 🔒 Authentication System  
✔ Secure **Login, Signup, Forget Password, and Reset Password** functionalities.  

### 🛍️ Product Experience  
✔ **Home Screen** – Explore collections, best sellers & shop by occasion.  
✔ **Best Seller Screen** – View trending products & easily add to cart.  
✔ **Product Details** – Get comprehensive product information & cart options.  
✔ **Categories & Occasions** – Filter products based on preferences & events.  

### 👤 User Profile & Address Management  
✔ Update & manage personal information.  
✔ **Google Maps Integration** for selecting & saving delivery addresses.  
✔ Dedicated screens for **active & completed orders**.  

### 🛒 Cart & Checkout  
✔ Add, update, or remove products from the cart.  
✔ Select delivery address & payment methods (**cash or credit**).  
✔ **Gift feature** – Send items as gifts! 🎁  

### 🌍 Localization & Accessibility  
✔ Full **Arabic & English** language support.  
✔ **Guest login** for easy exploration.  
✔ **Search functionality** for quick product discovery.  

### 🔔 Push Notifications  
✔ Receive updates on orders & exclusive offers!
